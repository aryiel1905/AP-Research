# AP Research privacy policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

AP Research is a free personal project for researching Australian suburbs. It is not sold and is not affiliated with the research websites it opens.

## Information the extension handles

- A postcode, chosen suburb, and newly saved suburb suggestions are kept in Chrome's local extension storage so the popup can remember them. The extension also stores panel display preferences locally.
- On the supported PropertyValue, SQM Research, Your Investment Property, realestate.com.au, and ABS QuickStats pages, the extension reads selected page content to show research figures and charts in its panels.
- When you choose to copy or download a chart, the extension may capture the visible tab, crop the chart locally, and place the resulting image on your clipboard or save it as a PNG. Copying a figure places that figure on your clipboard. Exporting suggestions saves a JSON file only when you request it.

The extension does not send this information to the developer or to a developer-operated server. It does not use analytics or advertising services. Opening a research website sends a normal browser request to that website, which is subject to that site's own privacy policy.

## Sharing and retention

AP Research does not sell or transfer user data to third parties for advertising or other unrelated purposes. Data saved in Chrome's local extension storage remains on your device until you change or remove it, clear the extension's data, or uninstall the extension. Clipboard content and downloaded files remain under your control.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/policies), including its Limited Use requirements.

For questions about this policy, open an issue in the [AP Research repository](https://github.com/aryiel1905/AP-Research/issues).
